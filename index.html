QuoteVoice

Speak the job. Send the quote.

QuoteVoice is a phone app for solar installers, electricians, plumbers and AC technicians. Describe the job out loud — in Urdu, English, Arabic or a mix — and QuoteVoice builds a priced quote from your own price list, makes a professional PDF and sends it to your customer on WhatsApp.

How it works

  • Speak: "Aslam sahab, Gulberg, 10 plate, inverter dedh lakh ka, 2 din ki mazdoori."
  • Check: the quote is filled in with your prices — edit anything in seconds.
  • Send: a clean PDF with materials, labour, total and amount in words goes out on WhatsApp.
  • Follow up: see when the customer opens it and get a reminder after 7 days.
