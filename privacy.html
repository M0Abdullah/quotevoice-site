Privacy policy

QuoteVoice · last updated 3 November 2026

This policy explains what the QuoteVoice app ("we") collects, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, and your name and email if you sign in with Google.
  • Business details you enter: business name, trade, phone, address, logo, currency, terms and your price list.
  • Quotes you create: customer name, customer phone number, site, items, prices and notes, and the text of what you said.
  • Voice recordings: when you record a job, the audio is sent to our server to be turned into text and a quote. We do not store the audio; it is discarded once the quote is returned.
  • Quote links: when a customer opens a quote link you sent, we record that it was opened and when.
  • Notification token: so we can tell you when a quote is opened and remind you to follow up.

Why we use it

Only to run the app: sign you in, build and store your quotes, create PDFs, sync your data between your phone and our server, and send you notifications. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

Services that process data for us

  • Supabase — account login, database and file storage.
  • Groq — converts your voice recording into text and a quote.
  • Google — "Continue with Google" sign-in.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile — protects sign-up from bots.
  • Expo and Firebase Cloud Messaging — deliver push notifications.
  • Google Play and RevenueCat — handle subscriptions, if you subscribe.

Your customers' details

Customer names and phone numbers are entered by you to create quotes. They are stored only in your account and used only to send that customer your quote.

Keeping your data safe

Your login session is stored in your phone's secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain). Data on our server is protected so that each account can only read its own records, and all traffic is encrypted.

Deleting your data

You can delete quotes and prices in the app at any time. To delete your account and all of its data, email us from your account's email address and we will delete it within 30 days.

Children

QuoteVoice is a business tool and is not meant for children under 13.
